Oct 2024 — Apr 2026

AI Engineer — Research

DT/IPL, TU Dortmund University

My research at TU Dortmund focused on making electronic design more intelligent and accessible. I developed an AI assistant that helps PCB designers by analyzing circuit files, visual layouts, and simulations. The system automates routine tasks and provides intelligent recommendations, cutting prototype review time by a quarter while significantly improving accuracy.

I also built a physics-informed model for predicting transmission line behavior on PCBs, combining reinforcement learning with genetic algorithms. This hybrid approach dramatically accelerated the design exploration process. Alongside this, I led work on explainable AI for healthcare, developing transparent models that predict sleep apnea episodes from patient sensor data with improved reliability and interpretability.

May 2022 — Sep 2024

At Wilo SE, I built a smart sensor system that transformed how the company monitors its industrial pump fleet. The predictive maintenance platform tracks over 100 pumps in real-time, detecting anomalies and predicting failures before they happen. The system significantly reduced signal processing time and improved fault prediction accuracy, giving maintenance teams the insights they need to prevent costly downtime.
